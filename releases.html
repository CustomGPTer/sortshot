1.3 18 November 2026

  • New in Settings: Timestamp on photos. The date and time are printed in the bottom-left corner of each photo as it is filed, the same size and place as the Samsung camera's own watermark.
  • New in Settings: Coordinates on photos. Where the photo was taken is printed on it as latitude and longitude, from the location the camera saved in the photo.
  • Both are off until you switch them on. They apply to JPEG photos; motion photos, HEIC, RAW, 200 MP photos and videos are filed without a stamp.

1.2 17 November 2026

First public release.

  • Pick a folder, tap Shoot, and the phone's own camera opens. What you take is filed into that folder when the camera closes.
  • Folders and sub-folders in DCIM › Sortshot, with readable file names.
  • Home-screen shortcuts for folders, a quick-settings tile and a Switch folder button while you shoot.
  • Tags and notes on one photo or many, dictated notes, and tags for a whole session before you shoot.
  • Tags and notes written into JPEG photos so they travel with them.
  • A review screen for each session's shots, and search across every folder.
  • Stamped copies, a PDF photo report and zip export.
  • Google Drive backup of chosen folders, over Wi-Fi, to your own Drive.
  • Hide folders from the Gallery, and a 30-day bin.

Every version is on the GitHub releases page.

Sortshot is free. No adverts, no accounts, no paid version.
