Privacy policy

Sortshot has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics. Its maker runs no servers and receives nothing from the app. Your photos, folders, tags and notes stay on your phone unless you switch on Google Drive backup, and then they go only to your own Drive.

When the app uses the internet

  • Checking for a new version. The app asks GitHub for the latest release. GitHub sees your internet address, as any website does. This can be switched off in Settings.
  • Google Drive backup, only if you connect it. Photos and videos in the folders you choose, and a list of your tags and notes, are sent to a folder called Sortshot in your own Google Drive. Sortshot asks Google for the narrowest permission there is: it can see and change only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive. Sign-in is handled by Google on your phone; Sortshot never sees your password. Disconnecting in the app gives the permission up. What is already in your Drive stays there until you remove it.
  • Dictating a note, only if you use it. The Dictate button opens the phone's own voice typing, which belongs to Google or Samsung and works under their terms. Sortshot receives only the words that come back.
  • Links you tap, such as this site or another app's page, open in your browser.

How Sortshot uses Google user data

With Google Drive connected, the app uses your Google account only to create, update, rename and move its own files and folders in your Drive, to read back its own list of tags and notes when you ask it to, and to show which account is connected. This data is not sent to the maker or to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train any model. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What is kept on the phone

  • Your photos and videos, as ordinary files in DCIM › Sortshot.
  • Your settings, and a small database holding tags, notes, what has been filed and what has been backed up.
  • The bin, in a hidden folder inside DCIM › Sortshot, for 30 days.
  • Reports and zips you make, in Documents › Sortshot.

Tags and notes are also written into JPEG photos themselves, so anyone you give such a photo to can read them. This can be switched off in Settings. Removing the app removes its settings and database. Your photos stay where they are.

What the permissions are for

  • All files access: to move the shots you take out of the Camera folder into your folders, and to manage those folders.
  • Usage access: to see which app is on screen, for one purpose: knowing the moment the camera closes so that filing stops. Nothing about your app use is stored or sent.
  • Notifications: for the Stop and Switch folder buttons while you shoot, the count of shots filed, and a notice if the Drive backup needs you to sign in again.
  • Internet: for the two uses above and nothing else.

Children

Sortshot is a general tool and is not aimed at children. It collects nothing from anyone.
