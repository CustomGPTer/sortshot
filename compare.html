What Sortshot does not do

Sortshot has no camera of its own and no photo editor. It is built for Samsung phones and works on Android 13 or later. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and it tells you when a new version is out.

Where the others do more

Folder Camera is on Google Play, so it updates itself. Utiful lets you bring folders in from a computer and send them back.
