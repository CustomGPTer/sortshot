The short version

Sortshot is free. Use it as you like. It moves your photos between folders and can write tags into them, so keep copies of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Using the app

  • The app is free. There is no paid version and nothing to buy in it.
  • Shots taken through Sortshot are moved from the Camera folder into the folder you chose. They are not copied.
  • Photos you delete in Sortshot go to its bin for 30 days. After that, or if you empty the bin, they cannot be brought back.
  • Photos sent to Google Drive are held under Google's terms, in your own account.
  • Use the app only on phones you own or are allowed to manage, and photograph only what you are entitled to.
